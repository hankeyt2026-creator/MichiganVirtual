Welcome

Listening to music has shaped me in ways I didn’t really notice at first, but looking back, it’s become one of the biggest influences on how I understand myself. When I actively seek out new songs — whether I’m digging through genres, following recommendations, or checking out my Discover Weekly on Spotify — I’m basically searching for pieces of myself in other people’s art. Certain tracks hit with a kind of emotional clarity I can’t always reach on my own. Sometimes a song says exactly what I’m feeling before I even know how to put it into words, and hearing that honesty makes those emotions easier to process instead of bottling them up. Music has also taught me to slow down and actually sit with what I’m feeling instead of brushing it off. Exploring different artists and sounds has opened me up creatively and made me more reflective, more patient, and more willing to understand the parts of myself that are messy or confusing. Even the simple act of scrolling through new releases or saving a song that resonates feels like choosing to grow a little. Over time, listening to music has made me more self-aware, more grounded, and more connected to the world around me.
